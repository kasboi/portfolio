Navigate is an open source project that helps users discover quality of life metrics and average salaries for various job sectors in urban areas around the world.

## Features

- Search for cities globally
- Filter by various criteria
- View city pictures and imagery
- Quality of life scoring
- Infinite scroll for seamless browsing
- Salary data for different job sectors
